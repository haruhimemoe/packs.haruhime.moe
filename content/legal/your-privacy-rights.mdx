## Your rights under the GDPR

- **Access and portability.** Get a copy of your data in a machine-readable file.
- **Rectification.** Have wrong data corrected.
- **Erasure.** Have your data deleted.
- **Restriction.** Ask us to pause using your data while a question about it is sorted out.
- **Objection.** Object to anything we do on the basis of legitimate interest.
- **Complaint.** Complain to your data protection supervisory authority.

### How to use them

- **Download my data** on your [account page](/me) gives you a JSON file with everything we hold about your account: your profile details, your linked osu! account record, your sessions, your saved packs, and your API key's prefix and dates.
- **Delete account** on the same page deletes your account, your sessions, your API key, and every pack you saved.
- Your name and avatar come from your osu! profile. Change them on osu!, and we copy the new ones the next time you sign in.
- For anything else, email [contact@haruhime.moe](mailto:contact@haruhime.moe). We answer within 30 days.

## Your rights under the CCPA

- **What we collect.** Identifiers (your osu! user ID, username, and IP address) and internet activity (request logs).
- **Selling and sharing.** We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't use it for ads.
- **Sensitive personal information.** We don't collect any.
- **Your rights.** You can ask to know what we hold, to delete it, and to correct it. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.
- **Opting out.** We honor Global Privacy Control signals. There's nothing to opt out of, because we don't sell or share your data.
- **Response time.** We answer within 45 days.

packs is small and free, and it may fall below the thresholds where the CCPA applies. We give these rights to everyone anyway, wherever you live.
