Archived pools are mappools from past osu! tournaments, saved as packs so you can open them, see their maps, and download them from the mirror like any other pack. On [Public packs](/packs) and on their own pages they have an "Archived pool" badge, and the "haruhime archive" account hosts them.

## Where they come from

The archived pools come from [otdb](https://otdb.sheppsu.me), the osu! tournament database by Sheppsu, used with permission. otdb collects pools from public tournament data: the pool's name, its slot labels and its beatmap IDs. We don't copy who submitted a pool to otdb.

Each archived pack links its pool on otdb, in its description and on its badge. The tournament, round and year come from the pool's name, so an unusual name can come out wrong. A pool whose slot labels we couldn't read was left out, never imported half right, and a pool listed twice is one pack.

## Finding them

On [Public packs](/packs), the Source filter shows Community packs, Archive packs, or both. Both are on at first.

## Used in N pools

On a pack's page and in the editor, a map that archived pools used says "Used in 3 pools" next to its stats. Press it to see which ones: the tournament and round, the year, and the slot the map was in, each linking its archived pack. The count leaves out the pack you're looking at, and a pool that used the map twice counts once.

Scripts and bots can get the same list from the [packs API](/docs/api), no key needed.

## Report a wrong one

If an archived pool has the wrong maps, slots, tournament or round, or shouldn't be listed at all, email [contact@haruhime.moe](mailto:contact@haruhime.moe) with the pack's link and what's wrong. We check it and hide or delete the pack if it's wrong. A pool we hid isn't imported again.
